## Accessing Noir Console

Noir Console is a cloud-based platform — no installation needed. Everything runs on our servers. You just need a browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Noir Console">
    Go to the URL provided by your administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Log in with the username and password given to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    After login, you'll see the main dashboard with all available modules in the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Your available modules depend on your subscription plan. If a module is not visible in the sidebar, contact your administrator.
</Info>
