## What is Noir Console?

Noir Console is a professional marketing automation tool for X (Twitter). It combines everything you need to run effective campaigns — from account management and content posting to audience scraping and direct message outreach.

### How It Works

Noir Console runs as a desktop application with a local backend server. All your accounts and data stay on your machine — nothing is sent to third-party servers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load your accounts">
    Add your X account tokens into the Token Manager and organize them into groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up proxies">
    Assign proxies to your accounts to protect them from bans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your workflow">
    Use any combination of modules — post content, boost engagement, scrape audiences, send DMs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor results">
    Track progress through live logs and task status across all modules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Always use proxies.** Running multiple accounts without proxies significantly increases the risk of account bans. We recommend assigning proxies to every account in Token Manager before using any other module.
</Warning>
