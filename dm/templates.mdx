## DM Templates

Templates let you create reusable message texts for your DM campaigns. Instead of typing the same message every time, create templates and select them when sending.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dm-templates.png" alt="DM Templates page" />
</Frame>

### Creating Templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Create Template'">
    Opens the template editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Type your DM text. Keep it natural and concise — overly long or spammy messages get ignored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Give the template a name and save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Template Groups

Organize templates into groups for different campaigns. For example:
- "Crypto outreach" — templates for token communities
- "Follow-up" — templates for replying to interested users

### Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Keep messages short">
    Short, direct messages get better response rates than long ones. 2-3 sentences max.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sound natural">
    Avoid obvious marketing language. Write as if you're sending a personal message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use multiple templates">
    Create several variations and rotate between them. This reduces the chance of X flagging your messages as spam.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
