## Bulk Sender

The Bulk Sender is the core of the DM module — it sends your template messages to all loaded targets using your DM accounts.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dm-bulk.png" alt="DM Bulk Sender page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ensure prerequisites">
    Before sending, make sure you have:
    - DM accounts attached and checked (DM → Accounts + Checker)
    - Targets loaded (DM → Targets)
    - At least one template created (DM → Templates)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select template">
    Choose which message template to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start sending">
    Click **Start**. Messages are distributed across all your DM accounts automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor progress">
    Watch the live progress — sent, failed, and remaining counts update in real-time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How It Works

The bulk sender distributes the workload across all your DM accounts:
- Each account sends messages simultaneously in each round
- All accounts fire at the same time per round, then wait for the next interval
- Failed messages are tracked separately

<Warning>
  **Sending speed is limited by X.** Don't expect thousands of DMs per minute — X throttles DM sending. Be patient and let the tool work at a sustainable pace to avoid account bans.
</Warning>

### Monitoring

Use the **Live Logs** panel (toggle in the header) to see detailed sending progress, including per-account status and any errors.
