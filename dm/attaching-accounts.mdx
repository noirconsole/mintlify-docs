## DM Accounts

Before sending DMs, you need to attach accounts that will be used as senders. These are separate from your main Token Manager pool — DM accounts need special configuration.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dm-accounts.png" alt="DM Accounts page" />
</Frame>

### Attaching Accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open DM → Accounts">
    Navigate to the DM section and click Accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add accounts">
    Paste auth_tokens of accounts you want to use for DM sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Run the DM Checker to confirm which accounts have active DM permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Not all X accounts can send DMs freely. Some accounts have DM restrictions due to age, activity level, or X's internal rules. Use the Checker to filter out restricted accounts.
</Info>

### Account Status

Each DM account shows its current state — whether it's verified for sending, has errors, or needs checking.

<Warning>
  **Always use proxies with DM accounts.** DM sending triggers the strictest anti-spam filters on X. Accounts sending DMs without proxies will get restricted very quickly.
</Warning>
