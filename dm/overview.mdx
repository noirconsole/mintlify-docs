## Overview

The DM module is a full outreach engine — attach accounts, build target lists, create templates, send in bulk, and monitor replies through Multichat.

### DM Campaign Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach DM accounts">
    Add accounts that will send the messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your targets">
    Run the DM Checker on your target database to filter only users with open DMs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load targets">
    Upload the filtered user IDs into Targets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create templates">
    Write message templates with optional variations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Launch the bulk sender — messages go out across all your DM accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Multichat">
    Check replies and engage with interested users.
  </Step>
</Steps>
