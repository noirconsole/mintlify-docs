### DM Campaign Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach DM accounts">
    Add accounts that will send the messages. These need active DM permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check accounts">
    Run the DM Checker to verify which accounts can actually send DMs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load targets">
    Upload user IDs from Scraping/Resolver, or load them manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create templates">
    Write message templates with optional variables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Launch the bulk sender — messages go out across all your DM accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor inbox">
    Check replies and engage with interested users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Use proxies and verified DM accounts.** DM sending is the most sensitive action on X. Accounts without proxies or without DM permission will fail or get banned.
</Warning>
