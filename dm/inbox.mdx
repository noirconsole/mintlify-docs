## DM Inbox

The Inbox shows replies from users who received your DMs. Monitor responses and engage with interested users directly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dm-inbox.png" alt="DM Inbox page" />
</Frame>

### Features

- **Conversation view** — see the full message thread with each user
- **Multi-account** — replies from all your DM accounts appear in one place
- **Real-time updates** — new replies appear as they come in

### Using the Inbox

Browse through conversations to find interested users. The inbox aggregates replies from all your DM sender accounts, so you don't need to check each account separately.

<Tip>
  Check the inbox regularly after launching a campaign. Quick replies to interested users convert better than delayed responses.
</Tip>
