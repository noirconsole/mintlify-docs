## DM Checker

The DM Checker is **not** for checking your sender accounts — it's for checking your **target database**. You load a list of usernames or IDs, and the checker filters it down to only users who have open DMs and can receive messages.

  <img src="/images/dm-checker.png" alt="DM Checker page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load your target database">
    Upload a file with usernames or user IDs — the list of people you plan to DM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the check">
    Click **Start**. The checker tests each user to see if their DMs are open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get filtered results">
    The checker outputs a cleaned list containing **only users with open DMs**. Users with closed DMs are removed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use in Bulk Sender">
    Load the filtered list into DM → Targets for your campaign. Now you know every target can actually receive your message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Why This Matters

Sending DMs to users with closed DMs wastes your sender accounts' rate limits and can trigger spam flags. By pre-filtering your database, you:
- Only send to users who can actually receive messages
- Don't waste your DM accounts on failed deliveries
- Get higher success rates on campaigns

<Tip>
  Always run the DM Checker before launching a bulk DM campaign. It saves time and protects your sender accounts.
</Tip>
