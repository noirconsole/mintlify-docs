## Raids (Manual Mode)

Raids let you send a burst of likes and retweets to specific tweets using your account pool. This is the manual mode — you pick the tweets and trigger the engagement.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smm-raids.png" alt="SMM Raids page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose which group of accounts will perform the engagement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste tweet URLs">
    Enter one or more tweet URLs to target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select actions">
    Choose what to do:
    - **Like** — all accounts like the tweet
    - **Retweet** — all accounts retweet
    - Both at once
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start raid">
    Click **Start**. All accounts in the group fire simultaneously per round.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Results

The progress display shows:
- **Success** — how many accounts completed the action
- **Failed** — how many failed (already liked, restricted, etc.)
- **Total** — total accounts in the group

<Info>
  If an account has already liked/retweeted a tweet, it will show as "failed" — this is normal and not an error.
</Info>
