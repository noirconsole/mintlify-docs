## Follow for Follow (F4F)

F4F automates the follow-for-follow pattern — your accounts follow users, and then unfollow those who don't follow back after a set period.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smm-f4f.png" alt="SMM F4F page" />
</Frame>

### How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose the accounts to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load targets">
    Provide a list of users to follow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure timing">
    Set how long to wait before checking if targets followed back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    The campaign follows targets, waits, then unfollows non-reciprocal accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Strategy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Target active accounts">
    Follow users who are active and likely to follow back — recently active accounts in your niche.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Don't rush unfollows">
    Give users enough time to see your follow and follow back before unfollowing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use proxies">
    Follow/unfollow cycles are heavily monitored by X. Proxies are essential.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
