## Auto Raid

Auto Raid monitors target accounts and **automatically raids their new posts** the moment they appear. Unlike manual raids where you pick specific tweets, Auto Raid watches accounts and reacts to new activity.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smm-auto.png" alt="SMM Auto Raid page" />
</Frame>

### How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose the accounts that will perform the engagement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add target accounts">
    Enter the usernames of accounts you want to monitor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking">
    Click **Start**. The system begins monitoring the target accounts for new posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic raid">
    When a target posts a new tweet, your accounts automatically like and retweet it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Use Cases

- **Boost your main account** — add your own account as a target. Every time you post, your pool automatically engages.
- **Track competitors** — monitor competitor accounts and immediately engage with their content to stay visible.
- **Support partners** — automatically boost posts from partner accounts.

<Info>
  Auto Raid runs continuously in the background. It checks for new posts at regular intervals and triggers the raid as soon as a new post is detected.
</Info>
