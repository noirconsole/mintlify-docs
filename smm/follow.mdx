## Follow

The Follow mode makes all accounts in your token group follow a single target user. This is useful for inflating follower count on a specific account or getting noticed by a target.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smm-follow.png" alt="SMM Follow page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose which accounts will do the following.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter target username">
    Enter a single username to follow (e.g., `elonmusk`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start**. All accounts in the group follow the target.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Results

The progress counter shows successful follows, failures, and total. Common failure reasons:
- Target account has blocked your account
- Target account is suspended
- Follow rate limit reached

<Warning>
  **Don't follow too aggressively.** X has strict follow limits. Spreading follows across more accounts with proxies gives better results than overloading a few accounts.
</Warning>
