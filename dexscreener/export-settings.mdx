## DexScreener Settings

Configure what tokens appear in the New Listings feed. Only tokens matching all your filters will be shown.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dex-settings.png" alt="DexScreener Settings page" />
</Frame>

### Monitor Toggle

Enable or disable DexScreener monitoring entirely. When disabled, no new tokens are tracked.

### Filter Options

| Setting | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Min Market Cap** | Minimum market cap in USD | $10,000 |
| **Max Market Cap** | Maximum market cap in USD | $1,000,000 |
| **Min Liquidity** | Minimum liquidity in USD | $5,000 |
| **Min Token Age** | Minimum age in minutes | 5 |
| **Max Token Age** | Maximum age in hours | 24 |
| **Chains** | Comma-separated list of chains to monitor | solana, ethereum, base, bsc |

### Saving

Click **Save** after making changes. A confirmation message appears when settings are saved successfully.

<Tip>
  Start with broader filters and narrow them down as you learn which tokens are most relevant to your campaigns.
</Tip>
