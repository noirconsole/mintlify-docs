## Followers Scraper

Scrape the follower list of any public X account. This is the primary way to build target lists for DM campaigns.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/followers-scraper.png" alt="Followers Scraper page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose a group from Token Manager. These accounts are used to perform the scraping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter target account">
    Paste the username or profile URL of the account whose followers you want to scrape.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start scraping">
    Click **Start**. The scraper will collect followers using your selected accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download results">
    Once complete, download the scraped usernames/IDs as a file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Use proxies on your scraping accounts.** Scraping makes many API calls and can trigger rate limits if all requests come from one IP.
</Warning>

### Tips

- Larger accounts (100k+ followers) take longer — the scraper paginates through the full list
- Use multiple accounts in your scraping group to speed up the process
- Scraped data can be fed directly into the **Resolver** (to get user IDs) or into **DM Targets**
