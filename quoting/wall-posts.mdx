## Wall Posts

Wall Posts broadcasts a message across multiple accounts' timelines simultaneously. Each account in your group posts the same text (or variations) as a new tweet on their own timeline.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/wall-posts.png" alt="Wall Posts page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose which accounts will post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write post text">
    Enter the text that each account will post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start**. Each account creates a new tweet with your text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Use Cases

- **Amplification** — post the same message from many accounts to flood a topic
- **Hashtag campaigns** — use a specific hashtag across many accounts
- **Content distribution** — spread a link or announcement across timelines

<Warning>
  Posting identical text from many accounts can trigger X's spam detection. Consider using slight variations in your text.
</Warning>
