## Reply Quotes

Reply Quotes posts replies under a target tweet using your accounts, with @mentions of other users in each reply. This creates engagement in the tweet's thread and notifies the mentioned users.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reply-quotes.png" alt="Reply Quotes page" />
</Frame>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose accounts that will post the replies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter tweet URL">
    Paste the URL of the tweet to reply to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add reply text">
    Write the reply text. Include @usernames to mention other users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load usernames to mention">
    Upload or paste a list of usernames to tag in the replies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start**. Each account posts a reply under the target tweet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How It Works

Each account from your group posts a reply comment under the target tweet. The replies include the custom text plus @mentions of users from your list. Mentioned users receive a notification, driving them to the tweet.

<Tip>
  Use usernames from the Scraping module as mention targets — scrape a community's followers, then mention them in replies to your content.
</Tip>
