## What is Quoting?

Quoting is a mass **@mention outreach** system. The core idea is simple: your accounts **mention target usernames** (`@user`) across different types of tweets. Every mention sends a **push notification** to the target user, driving them to see your content.

This is one of the most effective methods for gaining reach and attention on X — you are essentially putting your message directly into people's notification feeds.

### The Method

1. You load a **list of target usernames** (scraped from followers, communities, DexScreener exports, etc.)
2. You choose a **mention mode** — quote tweets, replies, image tags, or wall posts
3. Your accounts **mass-mention** those users by including `@username` in tweets
4. Each mentioned user gets a **notification from X** — they see your tweet, your link, your message
5. Result: **massive reach** without relying on the algorithm

<Info>
  The difference between modes is *where* the @mention appears — in a quote tweet, a reply, an image tag, or a standalone post. Each triggers a different type of notification on X, and each has different visibility and engagement patterns.
</Info>

## Four Modes

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Image Tags" icon="image" href="/quoting/image-tags">
    Post an image and tag users directly in it. Each tagged user gets a notification — the strongest attention signal on X.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quote Tags" icon="quote" href="/quoting/quote-tags">
    Quote-tweet a target tweet and include @mentions in the quote text. The mentioned users see your quote and the original tweet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reply Quotes" icon="reply" href="/quoting/reply-quotes">
    Reply under a tweet with @mentions and custom text. Targets get a notification and see the conversation thread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wall Posts" icon="clone" href="/quoting/wall-posts">
    Post a message with @mentions directly on your accounts' timelines. Targets get mentioned and your post appears in their notifications.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Comparison

| Mode | Notification Type | Visibility | Best For |
|------|------------------|------------|----------|
| **Image Tags** | "You were tagged in a photo" | High — image previews stand out | Maximum attention, visual content |
| **Quote Tags** | "Quoted your tweet" + mention | High — appears as a quote tweet | Promoting a specific tweet with context |
| **Reply Quotes** | "Mentioned you in a reply" | Medium — inside a thread | Engaging in existing conversations |
| **Wall Posts** | "Mentioned you" | Medium — standalone tweet | Broadcasting a message to many users |

### Typical Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scrape targets">
    Use the **Scraping** module to collect usernames from followers, communities, or DexScreener token holders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode">
    Pick the mention mode that fits your campaign — image tags for maximum impact, quote tags to promote a specific tweet, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select token group">
    Choose which accounts will do the mentioning. Use the **Token Manager** to organize them into groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your text and load targets">
    Write the tweet text and load the list of @usernames to mention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Start the campaign. Your accounts distribute the mentions across the target list automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always use **proxies** with your accounts. Mass mentioning without proxies will lead to account restrictions. See [Token Manager — Proxies](/token-manager/proxies).
</Warning>
