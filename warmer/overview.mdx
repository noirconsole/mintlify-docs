## Warmer

The Warmer simulates natural account activity — browsing, liking, and interacting — to keep your accounts healthy and avoid restrictions from X's anti-bot systems.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/warmer.png" alt="Warmer page" />
</Frame>

### Why Warm Accounts

New or inactive accounts are more likely to be flagged by X. The Warmer makes your accounts look active and natural before using them for campaigns.

<Warning>
  **Warm accounts before heavy use.** Using fresh accounts for DM sending or mass following without warming increases the risk of bans.
</Warning>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up warmer accounts">
    Go to **Warmer → Accounts** to configure which accounts will be warmed and assign proxies to each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a warming task">
    On the Dashboard, create a new task — select accounts and set activity parameters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Launch the task. Accounts begin performing natural-looking activity (browsing timelines, liking tweets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Track progress on the dashboard via task cards showing status, account count, and progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Multi-Task

You can run multiple warming tasks simultaneously — each targeting different account groups with different parameters. Tasks appear as cards on the dashboard where you can pause, resume, edit, or delete them.

Tasks persist across server restarts and resume automatically.

<Tip>
  Warm new accounts for at least a few days before using them for campaigns. The longer the warm-up, the safer the accounts.
</Tip>
