## Feed View

The feed shows posts from all your tracked lists in a unified timeline, similar to your X home feed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/lists-feed.png" alt="Lists feed view" />
</Frame>

### What You See

Each post in the feed shows:
- **Author** — profile picture, display name, username
- **Post text** — full tweet content with expanded URLs
- **Media** — images and videos if attached
- **Quote tweet cards** — if the post is a quote tweet, the quoted content is shown inline
- **Retweet cards** — retweets show the original tweet content
- **Engagement stats** — likes, retweets, replies count
- **Timestamp** — when the post was made

### Usage

The feed is useful for:
- **Monitoring competitors** — track lists of competitor accounts
- **Finding content** — discover tweets worth engaging with
- **Research** — keep an eye on specific communities or niches
