## What Are Groups?

Groups let you organize your accounts by purpose. Instead of selecting individual tokens every time, you create a group once and reuse it across all modules.

**Examples of how groups are used:**
- "DM Senders" — accounts dedicated to sending direct messages
- "Boost Pool" — accounts that like/retweet your main account's posts
- "SMM Workers" — accounts for follow campaigns

<Info>
  Groups are referenced throughout the app. When you see a **group dropdown** in any module (DM, SMM, Quoting, Posting Tool, Warmer), it pulls from the groups you create here.
</Info>

## Creating a Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Create Group'">
    Opens the group creation dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your group">
    Give it a descriptive name (e.g., "DM Accounts", "Boost Team").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Two ways to add accounts:
    - **Pick from Pool** — select from your existing validated accounts
    - **Paste tokens** — paste auth_tokens directly (they'll be added to the pool automatically)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The group is now available in all module dropdowns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Groups

Each group card shows:
- **Member count** — how many accounts are in the group
- **Validation status** — how many are valid/invalid

### Group Actions

- **Validate group** — check all accounts in this group
- **Add/remove members** — edit the group's account list
- **Delete group** — remove the group (accounts stay in the pool)

<Warning>
  Deleting a group does **not** delete the accounts from the pool. It only removes the grouping.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Create separate groups for each workflow">
    Don't use the same accounts for DM sending and SMM raids. Different workflows have different risk profiles — keep them isolated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Validate groups regularly">
    Tokens expire over time. Run validation on your groups periodically to ensure all members are still active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Name groups clearly">
    Use descriptive names that tell you at a glance what the group is for. Avoid generic names like "Group 1".
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
