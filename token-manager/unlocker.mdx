## What Is Unlocker?

Unlocker lets you change the privacy setting of your X accounts in bulk:
- **Make Public** (unlock) — anyone can see the account's tweets
- **Make Private** (protect) — only approved followers can see tweets

This is useful when preparing accounts for campaigns — public accounts are needed for posting, quoting, and engagement activities.

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select accounts">
    Choose a group from the dropdown, or select all accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose action">
    Click either:
    - **Unlock** (shield-open icon) — make accounts public
    - **Protect** (shield icon) — make accounts private
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    After the operation completes, you'll see success/failed/total counts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
