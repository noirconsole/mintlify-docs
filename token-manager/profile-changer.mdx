## What Is Profile Changer?

Profile Changer lets you update display names and profile pictures (avatars) across many accounts in a single operation.

**Use cases:**
- Giving your boost accounts unique-looking profiles
- Refreshing account identities for new campaigns
- Making accounts look more natural with real names and photos

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token source">
    Choose how to select accounts:
    - **Select Group** — pick a group from the dropdown
    - **Paste Tokens** — paste auth_tokens directly
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add display names">
    Enter names in the text area — one name per line. Names are assigned to accounts in order.
    ```
    John Smith
    Alex Johnson
    Maria Garcia
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload avatars (optional)">
    Upload multiple image files. Avatars are assigned to accounts in order, matching the name list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start** to begin the bulk profile update. The progress counter shows success/failed/total.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The token count indicator shows how many accounts will be updated, and how many names you've provided. Make sure the numbers match.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Profile changes are visible immediately on X. Changing too many profiles too quickly without proxies may trigger rate limits.
</Warning>
