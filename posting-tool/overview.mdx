## Posting Tool (Post & Boost)

The Posting Tool publishes a post from your main account and **instantly boosts it** with likes and retweets from your account pool. The goal is to push your post to the **Top of Search** on X.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/posting-tool.png" alt="Posting Tool page" />
</Frame>

### Why This Works

When you include hashtags or cashtags in your post (e.g., `$JUP`, `$AURA`, `#crypto`), X indexes it in search results. If your post gets immediate engagement (likes + retweets from your boost pool), it rises to the **top of search results** for that tag.

**Example:** You post a tweet containing `$JUP` → your boost pool instantly likes and retweets it → when someone searches `$JUP` on X, your post appears at the top.

<Tip>
  Use cashtags like `$JUP`, `$AURA`, `$SOL` or hashtags like `#memecoin` in your posts. The boost pushes them to the top of search, making your content visible to everyone searching for that token or topic.
</Tip>

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your main account">
    Choose the account that will publish the post — either from the dropdown or paste a token directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select boost group">
    Choose a token group that will automatically like and retweet your post after publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your post">
    Enter the post text. Include `$cashtags` or `#hashtags` for search visibility. Optionally attach images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post & Boost">
    Click **Post**. The tool publishes from your main account, then your boost group immediately engages with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Task Management

Each post creates a task that you can manage:
- **Stop** — cancel a running task
- Tasks persist across server restarts — they resume automatically
