## Overview

The Posting Tool (Post & Boost) lets you post content from your main account and automatically boost it with likes/retweets from your account pool. This is the core content distribution feature.

### How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your main account">
    Choose the account that will publish the post. You can select from a dropdown or paste a token directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure boost groups">
    Select which account groups will automatically like and retweet your post after publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your post">
    Add text and optionally attach images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post & Boost">
    The tool publishes from your main account, then triggers the boost group to engage with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
