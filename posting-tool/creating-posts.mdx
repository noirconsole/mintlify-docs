### Setting Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select main account">
    At the top, choose which account will publish the post:
    - **Dropdown** — select from your validated accounts
    - **Paste token** — paste an auth_token directly
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select boost group">
    Choose a token group that will automatically like/retweet the post after it's published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write post text">
    Enter your tweet text in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach images (optional)">
    Upload one or more images to include in the post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Click **Post**. The tool publishes from your main account, then the boost group engages with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The boost happens automatically after posting — you don't need to manually trigger it.
</Info>
